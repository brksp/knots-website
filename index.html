Knots

The knots sailors actually use. Type what you need to do and learn the right knot step by step.

Coming soon to the App Store

What's inside

  • What do I need? Type "hang a fender" or tap a situation to get the right knot for the job, and why.
  • Step-by-step animation Jump to any step, slow it down, skip to the tightening. Left-handed mode mirrors the animation.
  • Practice Test yourself with Identify, Order and Situation modes.
  • Spaced review Knots you learn come back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, so they stick.
  • Works offline Everything ships inside the app, so it works at anchor, offshore and anywhere without signal.
